# Vinkius

> The AI connectivity layer for your everyday apps. Vinkius is the AI Connectivity Cloud: 9,258 managed Connectors exposing 63,093 capabilities that AI agents call directly. One URL per Connector works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor and 16 more AI clients, with every connection authenticated, authorized and managed in one place. Every Connector ships a prerendered page plus a step-by-step setup guide for each supported client. Available in English, Portuguese, French, Spanish, Japanese and German.
